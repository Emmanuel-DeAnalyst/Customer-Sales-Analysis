**Project Category:** Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Customer Sales Analysis

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze customer sales performance from 2021 to 2023. The dashboard explores revenue trends, purchasing behaviour, customer demographics, shopping mall performance, product categories, and payment methods.

Using interactive filters and visualizations, the analysis provides insights into customer spending patterns, revenue distribution, and business performance to support informed decision-making and sales strategy optimization.

## 📈 Key Performance Highlights

- **Total Revenue:** $251.51M
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 298,712 units
- **Average Price:** $689.26
- **Average Customer Age:** 43.43 years
- **Top Shopping Malls:** Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon, generating approximately $51M each.
- **Leading Product Category:** Clothing, contributing approximately $114M in revenue.
- **Gender Distribution:** Female customers accounted for 59.72% of revenue, while male customers contributed 40.28%.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Customer Demographics
- Analyzed customer distribution by gender and age group.
- Compared purchasing patterns across adult, young adult, and older customer segments.
- Identified differences in customer representation to support audience segmentation.

### 2. Shopping Mall Performance
- Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon ranked highest in revenue, generating approximately $51M each.
- Metrocity followed with approximately $37M.
- Compared revenue contributions across multiple shopping malls to identify leading retail locations.

### 3. Product Category Analysis
- Clothing generated the highest revenue at approximately $114M.
- Shoes contributed approximately $67M.
- Technology generated approximately $58M.
- Other categories included cosmetics, toys, food and beverages, books, and souvenirs.

### 4. Payment Method Analysis
- **Cash:** $113M
- **Credit Card:** $88M
- **Debit Card:** $51M

Cash transactions accounted for the largest share of revenue, followed by credit and debit card payments.

### 5. Yearly Revenue Trends
- Revenue remained relatively stable between 2021 and 2022 at approximately $115M annually.
- Revenue declined to approximately $22M in 2023, highlighting a significant change that warrants further investigation.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- KPI cards for total revenue, quantity sold, average price, and average customer age.
- Revenue analysis by gender and age group.
- Shopping mall revenue comparison.
- Product category performance analysis.
- Revenue distribution by payment method.
- Year-over-year revenue trend analysis.
- Interactive filters for month, shopping mall, and product category.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard transforms raw customer transaction data into actionable business insights. It enables stakeholders to evaluate sales performance, understand customer demographics, identify high-performing retail locations and product categories, and assess payment preferences.

These insights can support customer segmentation, product planning, retail performance evaluation, and data-driven sales strategies.
